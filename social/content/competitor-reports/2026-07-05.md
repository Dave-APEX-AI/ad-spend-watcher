## Format & hook trends (early July 2026)

- **Algorithm (Mosseri-confirmed):** the three ranking signals are **watch time, sends-per-
  reach (DM shares), likes-per-reach.** Sends weighted **3–5× likes**; **saves ~3× likes.**
  Build reels people *send to a mate* and carousels people *save*.
  (https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-algorithms/)
- **Originality penalty is now teeth, not warning:** original content gets **40–60% more
  distribution**; **10+ reposts in 30 days = excluded from recommendations entirely.** Our
  faceless-but-original reels are on the right side of this.
- **Raw > polished:** unpolished content is out-reaching high-production. Keep the render
  clean but the *energy* raw/direct.
- **Length:** completion rate is king; 15–30s for tight utility reels, longer (up to 3 min)
  can now reach non-followers if watch-time holds. Don't pad.
- **Hooks:** text-on-first-frame non-negotiable — 50% drop in first 3s kills reach.
- **Audio:** "Game Time" (Future & Tyla, World Cup) still live but **decaying** — use now or
  skip. "Bed On Fire" (G Flip) rising for summer.
  ⚠️ **OPERATIONAL CAVEAT (log this):** business/creator accounts have a **restricted music
  library** — many trending songs are personal-account only and will **mute the reel or cap
  reach** if used. For @caillte_ai use tracks labelled **"Original audio"** or our own bed.
  This is why our reels ship a silent/own audio track by default — correct call, keep it.
  (https://buffer.com/resources/trending-audio-instagram/)
